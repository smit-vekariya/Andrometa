Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Here's exactly what we collect, why, and what we never touch.

Last updated: 1 January 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to GoPlus Drive ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and safeguard your information when you use the GoPlus Drive mobile application ("App"). Please read this policy carefully.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Google account email addresses you connect to the App.
  • Contact messages submitted via our contact form (name, email, message).

2.2 Automatically Collected

  • Anonymised app usage analytics (features used, crash reports).
  • Device information: OS version, device model, app version.

2.3 What We Do Not Collect

  • Your Google account password — authentication is handled solely by Google OAuth 2.0.
  • The content of files stored in your Google Drive.
  • Your precise GPS location.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the GoPlus Drive service.
  • To display and manage files across connected Google accounts.
  • To improve performance and fix bugs via crash analytics.
  • To respond to support and contact enquiries.

4. Google API & OAuth

GoPlus Drive uses the Google Drive API and Google OAuth 2.0. We request only the minimum permissions necessary and comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy. Your Google credentials are never stored on our servers.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information. We may share data only with trusted service providers helping us operate the App, or if required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service. Removing accounts from the App results in data purge within 30 days.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption in transit and secure token storage.

8. Children's Privacy

GoPlus Drive is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we discover we have, we will delete it immediately.

9. Your Rights

  • Access, correct or delete personal data we hold.
  • Revoke Google OAuth access via your Google Account settings at any time.

Contact privacy@goplusdrive.app to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be notified via the App or email. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact

Questions? Email privacy@goplusdrive.app or visit our Contact page.
