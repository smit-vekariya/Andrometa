Features

Built for people with multiple Google accounts

GoPlus Drive gives you one unified view across all your Google Drive storage — simple, fast, and secure.

Smart Storage Switching

Automatically changes storage and routes files to the account with the most available space, acting as a combined database.

Multi-Drive Search

Search across all your connected drives at once. Find any file instantly regardless of which account securely holds it.

Google Drive Integration

Seamlessly integrate your existing Google Drive accounts via secure OAuth. Unified view of all uploaded Google files.

OneDrive Integration

Connect your Microsoft OneDrive accounts alongside Google Drive, combining cloud ecosystems into one powerful app.

End-to-End Encryption

Your privacy is prioritized. Sensitive data is protected with secure encryption, ensuring no unauthorized access or leaks.

Fast File Downloads

Download your files instantly to your local device directly from any connected cloud drive with maximum efficiency.

Auto Sync & Backup

Never worry about losing your data. Enjoy automatic background syncing to securely backup photos, videos, and documents.

Move to Cloud

Instantly backup files right from your native file manager. Use the 'Move to Cloud' direct share option for quick uploads.

Secure File Sharing

Easily share your files or folders globally. Generate precise links tailored with custom access permissions to external users.

Duplicate File Finder

Automatically scan to detect duplicate files that are filling up your connected drives and safely remove the redundant copies.

Secure Sensitive Vault

Maintain your absolute privacy. Use the built-in vault feature to hide and lock specific sensitive files from being viewed publicly.

Smart Notifications

Stay thoroughly updated on upload completions, storage limits getting near, and shared file activity right strictly on your phone.

Auto Format Converter

Convert your files on the go efficiently! Supports transforming PDF to Word or Word to PDF without requiring external tools.

Unified Trash Bin

Move unwanted files dynamically to the bin safely. Don't worry about accidental deletes, as trash lets you quickly recover them.

Storage Clean Up

Analyze your overall account usage deeply and cleanly remove large or unnecessary caches to immediately clear up storage space.

How it works

Up and running in 3 steps

No technical setup needed. If you use Google Drive, you're already ready.

1

Install the App

Download GoPlus Drive from the Google Play Store and open it on your Android device.

2

Tap "Add Account" and sign in via secure OAuth. Add as many accounts as you need.

3

Use as One Drive

All your storage merges instantly. Upload, browse and share from one single interface.
